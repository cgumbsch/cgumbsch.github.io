Research interest

Inspired by how humans learn and make decisions, my research aims to build safe, generalist embodied agents that think before they act. To get there, I work on three building blocks:


1. Imagine: world models. Agents that predict possible outcomes of their actions using structured and hierarchical representations of how the world works, e.g., THICK, GateL0RD.


2. Verify: vision-language reward models. Agents that use vision-language models to judge whether outcomes, imagined or observed, meet a goal, e.g., SENSEI, Demo2Reward.


3. Act: planning and policies. Agents that act through both hierarchical, temporally abstract planning and reactive policies such as VLAs, e.g., L0RD CAPRI, Hierarchical RL perspective.
